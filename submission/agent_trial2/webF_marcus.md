# Web test F: Marcus (badminton organiser, laptop 1280x900)

## What he did
Signed up (demo), started "Thursday Shuttlers", posted Thu 19:00 for 3. Ran a Huddle (badminton, 4, "Shows up 75%+"), then opened Courts & prices, How you play (one A/B answer, "people nearby", trust and chain) and the Agent home, and typed a request to the agent.

## What worked
- The club and the game took about a minute each, and the game card is clear.
- The huddle thread builds trust: it says why Dan and Grace were left out, and the venue haggle (NT$410 to NT$320) is a nice touch.
- The Courts heatmaps are useful, and How you play updates as soon as he answers.

## Problems, worst first
1. **Agents never fill his game.** The posted club game stayed at "Nobody yet · 3 left". The huddle made a separate game (Thu 5 Nov **07:00**, Taipei Gymnasium) that stayed "AGREED, ASKING" for more than 5 minutes. He never saw who was in: back to the group chat.
2. **No day or time in the Huddle form.** It picked 07:00 in the morning, while How you play says "You like Thursday evenings".
3. **The demo account carries other testers' state.** YOUR GAMES lists "Sunday 1 Nov 19:30 ... with Leo, 2 of 3 in", which he never joined. The chat and huddle list hold items he never made.
4. **A basketball match for a badminton player.** A toast said "Your agent found a game: Basketball" when he had no agent and no sports picked.
5. **Distances disagree.** With location denied, Courts only says "Share your area first" and offers no map pin. Yet the huddle said "Your trip 1.8 km", and Courts later put the same gym at 3.2 km.
6. **Club page gaps.** The owner gets only a "Leave" button: no invite link, no members list. The place he typed is not shown. The game form asks him to pin the place again.
7. **Layout.** The mouse wheel over a map zooms it instead of scrolling the page. Agent home jumps while loading, so his first typing was lost.
8. **The agent ignored "reliable"** and did not warn about the duplicate game. The chain link opens raw JSON.

## Confusing wording
- "Shows up 75%+" next to "keeps under 80% of its yeses": two percentages side by side.
- The Huddle form says "New people are let in", but How you play says "90% games wait-list new people".
- Venue names mix languages: 松山運動中心, 大安運動中心, "Daan Sports Center", "Badminton court".

## Top 3 fixes
1. Let the Huddle fill the posted game at its day, time and gate, and end with a settled "3 of 3 in: Ivan, Lily, Leo" card.
2. Add day and time to the Huddle form, respect the learned evening preference, and never suggest sports he hasn't picked.
3. Give each demo sign-up its own state, and make trip distances agree across screens (with pin-on-map when location is denied).

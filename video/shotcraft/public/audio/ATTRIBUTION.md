# Audio in the demo video

Every sound in the film (`src/v3/Main3.tsx`, and the older `src/Main.tsx`) is one of the files below, each from Mixkit
under its free licenses (commercial use, no attribution required), with the item it was downloaded from. On 1 November
2026 five earlier files whose source could not be traced (a background track and four effects) were replaced with
these, and the film was rendered again.

The narration is ours: written by us and spoken by Kokoro (Apache 2.0), on our own server.
